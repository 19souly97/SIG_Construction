Construction is all we do.

Solace Construction PLC is an Ethiopian contractor focused exclusively on building and infrastructure works — no side businesses, no divided attention.

Our story

From one excavator to 620 people on site.

We began in 2008 doing what nobody else wanted: excavation and substructure on difficult Addis Ababa plots. That reputation for getting the hard part right is still the foundation of the company.

Today we self-perform the full structural package and coordinate MEP and finishing in-house, which means a single point of accountability from the first pour to the final snag.

  • Grade 1 General Contractor

    Licensed for unlimited-value building and road contracts in Ethiopia.

  • ISO-aligned QA/QC

    Documented inspection and test plans for every structural and MEP activity.

  • Owned plant fleet

    Tower cranes, batching plants, pumps and aluminium formwork systems.
