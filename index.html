Who we are

A contractor built around one discipline: construction.

Solace Construction PLC is an Ethiopian building and infrastructure contractor. We are not a diversified holding — every engineer, foreman and machine in our organisation exists to put quality structures in the ground and hand them over on time.

  • Self-performed structure

    In-house formwork, rebar and concrete crews — no subcontracting the critical path.

  • Owned plant & equipment

    Tower cranes, batching plants and formwork systems available when the programme needs them.

  • Zero-compromise HSE

    Daily toolbox talks, certified scaffolding and a 2.1M safe man-hour record.
